Advanced Sol Language Manual

--- Macros:
Create:
#MACRO NAME
    ...
#MACRO

Usage:
NAME

--- Macros with args:
#MACRO IMM8
IMMH #ARG2
IMML #ARG3
IMM #ARG1
#MACRO

IMM8 ACC 2 F

Output:
IMMH 2
IMML F
IMM ACC

--- Imports:
for example:
- mylib.asl
#MACRO IMM8
IMMH #ARG2
IMML #ARG3
IMM #ARG1
#MACRO

- myprogram.asl
#IMPORT mylib.asl

IMM8 ACC F F
